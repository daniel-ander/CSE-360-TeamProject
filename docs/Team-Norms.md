# CSE360 Group Project: Team Norms

## Facilitators
- Every week we will alternate facilitators if the team requires one.  
- At the start of the week, if we feel we need a facilitator, we will take volunteers so everyone gets the experience.  
- Multiple facilitators may serve at once for additional experience.  

## Workload & Assignments
- All group members will make their best effort to ensure the workload is spread evenly.  
- We will volunteer for project assignments.  
- In the event of an irresolvable situation, we will defer to the facilitator.  
  - If no facilitator has been appointed, one will be chosen.  

## Decision Making
- Team decisions will be voted on using Discord polls.  
- Since we have 5 members, votes should not end in a tie.  

## Deadlines & Check-Ins
- By the **mid-deadline**, everyone should know their roles and have a general idea of how to tackle their portion.  
- Around the mid-deadline, we will do a quick check-in to confirm expectations and progress.  
- This ensures enough time to address problems before the final deadline.  

## Work Quality
- We all agree to produce and expect **A-level work**.  

## Communication
- We will communicate via **Discord**.  
- All members will try to respond within **24 hours at the latest**.  
- Nobody should need to be tracked down.  

## Emergencies
- In case of emergency, the affected member will turn in any completed work.  
- The rest of the team agrees to step up and handle what remains.  
- The emergency should be communicated as soon as possible.  

## Problem Solving
- Problems will be addressed as a group in a group setting.  
- If unresolved, we will use a **group vote via Discord polls**.  
- If still unresolved, we will seek outside guidance from the professor.  

## Accountability
- If a member is not pulling their weight, producing poor quality work, or another grievance arises, it will be addressed as a group.  
- If a member knows they cannot deliver their share, they must alert the team so arrangements can be made.  
- If unresolved, we will escalate to the professor.  

## Consequences
- Violations of team rules may result in:  
  - Being left off of completed assignments or the project.  
  - Referral to the professor with logs/evidence.  
- Everyone will have a few **“Strikes”** allowed to account for small mistakes or unavoidable issues.
